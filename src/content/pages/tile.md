## Signs of a tile roof leak

- **Cracked, broken or slipped tiles** you can see from the ground
- **Ceiling stains**, often after a long, heavy rain
- **Tiles that look out of line** or have gaps between them
- **Damp wood or insulation** in the attic
- **Leaks around chimneys, skylights or roof valleys**

## Why tile roofs leak

Many people think a tile roof can't leak because tile lasts so long. But the tile is only half the system. Under it is a layer of underlayment that does most of the waterproofing. Here's what usually goes wrong:

**Worn underlayment.** Years of Florida heat break it down. Once it cracks or tears, water that gets past the tile goes straight to the deck.

**Cracked or slipped tiles.** A broken tile lets far more water reach the underlayment than it was built to handle.

**Valleys and flashing.** Metal valleys and wall flashing can rust or get packed with debris, so water backs up under the tile. See [flashing and vent leak repair](/roof-flashing-leak-repair/).

**Ridge and hip tiles.** The caps along the top and edges are set in mortar or foam. If that fails, the caps loosen and water gets in.
