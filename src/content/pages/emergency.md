## Water coming through the ceiling? Do this first

1. **Call us.** We pick up 24/7 and can talk you through the next steps.
2. **Protect what's inside.** Move electronics, files and stock. Put down buckets and towels.
3. **Stay safe.** Keep people away from sagging ceilings. Shut off power near wet lights or outlets.
4. **Stay off the roof** during wind, rain or lightning. We will handle it.

## What causes emergency flat roof leaks in Orlando

Most emergency calls we get come after a hard storm. Central Florida gets heavy rain almost every summer afternoon, plus tropical storms and hurricanes. That puts a lot of stress on flat roofs.

- **Wind damage:** seams pulled open or flashing torn loose
- **Fallen branches or debris** that puncture the roof
- **Clogged drains** that let water pile up until it finds a way in
- **Old patches** that finally give out under heavy rain
- **HVAC work** where a technician cut or damaged the roof

## How we handle an emergency leak

First we stop the water. That might mean a temporary patch, sealing an open seam or clearing a blocked drain. Then we document the damage with photos. Once the roof is dry, we come back and make a permanent repair backed by a written warranty.

If you don't know where the water is getting in, we use [flat roof leak detection](/flat-roof-leak-detection/) to track it down. Leaks on flat roofs often show up far from where they start.
