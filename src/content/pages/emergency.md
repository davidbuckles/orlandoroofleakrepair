## Water coming through the ceiling? Do this first

1. **Call us.** We pick up 24/7 and can talk you through the next steps.
2. **Protect what's inside.** Move electronics, papers and furniture. Put down buckets and towels.
3. **Stay safe.** Keep people away from sagging ceilings. Shut off power near wet lights or outlets.
4. **Stay off the roof** during wind, rain or lightning. We will handle it.
5. **Take photos** of the water and damage inside for your insurance company.

## What causes emergency roof leaks in Orlando

Most emergency calls come after a hard storm. Central Florida gets heavy rain almost every summer afternoon, plus tropical storms and hurricanes.

- **Wind damage:** shingles torn off, tiles lifted, metal trim bent back or flat roof seams pulled open
- **Fallen trees and branches** that crack tile or punch through the roof
- **Clogged gutters, valleys or drains** that let water back up under the roof
- **Old pipe boots and patches** that finally give out in heavy rain
- **Rooftop work** where an AC tech, satellite installer or other trade damaged the roof

## How we handle an emergency leak

First we stop the water. That might mean a tarp, a temporary patch, sealing a seam or clearing a blocked drain. Then we document the damage with photos. Once the roof is dry, we come back and make a permanent repair backed by a written warranty.
