## Signs your roof is leaking

A roof leak doesn't always drip right away. Water can run along the deck, rafters or insulation and show up far from where it got in. Watch for these signs:

- **Ceiling stains** or bubbling paint, even small ones
- **Missing, lifted or cracked shingles** after a storm
- **Cracked or slipped tiles** on a tile roof
- **Loose screws or rust** on a metal roof
- **Ponding water** on a flat roof two days after rain
- **Damp spots in the attic**, dark wood or wet insulation
- **Musty smells** or mold near the ceiling

Orlando's afternoon storms, summer heat and hurricane season are hard on every type of roof. A small leak today can mean rotted decking, mold and a much bigger bill in a few months.
