## Signs your flat roof is leaking

Flat roofs don't always drip right away. Water can travel under the roof and show up far from where it got in. Watch for these signs:

- **Ceiling stains** or bubbling paint, even small ones
- **Ponding water** that sits on the roof for more than two days after rain
- **Blisters or bubbles** in the roof surface
- **Split or lifting seams**, especially near HVAC units and vents
- **Musty smells** or mold near the top floor
- **Wet insulation** or soft spots when you walk on the roof

Orlando's afternoon storms and summer heat are hard on flat roofs. Heat makes the roof expand. Rain finds every weak spot. A small leak today can mean wet insulation, mold and a much bigger bill in a few months.

We only work on flat and low-slope roofs, so we know where they fail.

- **Modified bitumen:** cracked laps, blisters and worn torch-down. See [modified bitumen repair](/modified-bitumen-roof-leak-repair/).
- **Built-up roofs (tar and gravel):** cracks, bare spots and failed patches
- **Roof coatings:** peeling or worn coating that no longer keeps water out

- **Drains and scuppers:** clogged, cracked or pulled-loose drains. See [roof drain and flashing repair](/roof-drain-flashing-leak-repair/).
