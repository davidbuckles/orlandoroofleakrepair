## Signs your flat roof is leaking

- **Ponding water** two days or more after rain
- **Blisters or bubbles** in the roof surface
- **Split or lifting seams**, often near HVAC units
- **Cracks or bare spots** in the surface
- **Ceiling stains** or falling ceiling tiles inside
- **Soft spots** when you walk on the roof

## Flat roofs we repair

**TPO.** White or gray single-ply sheets with heat-welded seams. Most leaks start at open seams or punctures. See [TPO roof leak repair](/tpo-roof-leak-repair/).

**Modified bitumen.** Rolled asphalt roofing, often torched on. Leaks start at cracked laps, blisters and worn spots. See [modified bitumen roof leak repair](/modified-bitumen-roof-leak-repair/).

**Built-up roofs.** Layers of tar and felt topped with gravel. They leak where the gravel has worn away or the layers crack.

**Coated roofs.** A coating can stop leaks for years, but once it peels or wears thin, water gets through.

## Where flat roofs really leak

The open middle of a flat roof rarely fails first. Most leaks start at drains, scuppers, HVAC curbs, parapet walls and pipes. See [flashing, vent and roof drain leak repair](/roof-flashing-leak-repair/).
