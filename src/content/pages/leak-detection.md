## Why roof leaks are hard to find

Water moves. On a sloped roof, it can slip under shingles or tile, run down the underlayment and drip off a rafter far from where it got in. On a flat roof, it spreads sideways under the surface and soaks the insulation. Many people pay for a patch over the stain, only to have the leak come back.

## How we find the leak

**Attic inspection:** On homes, we start inside. Stains, damp wood and wet insulation point us to the area of the roof to check.

**Roof inspection:** We check the spots where most leaks begin: pipe boots, flashing, valleys, skylights, chimneys, screws, seams and drains.

**Water testing:** When the source still isn't clear, we run water over one small section of roof at a time while someone watches inside. When the drip starts, we've found it.

**Infrared scan and moisture survey:** On flat roofs, an infrared camera after sunset shows wet areas under the surface. Moisture meters confirm how far the water has spread.
