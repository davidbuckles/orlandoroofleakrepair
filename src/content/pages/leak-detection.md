## Why flat roof leaks are hard to find

On a sloped roof, water runs downhill. On a flat roof, it can spread sideways under the membrane, soak the insulation and drip out somewhere else. Many people pay for a patch over the stain, only to have the leak come back. Finding the real source first saves money.

## How we find the leak

**Visual inspection:** We start on the roof. We check seams, flashing, drains, HVAC curbs and pipe boots, where most leaks begin.

**Infrared scan:** Wet insulation holds the day's heat longer than dry insulation. An infrared camera after sunset shows these warm spots. It helps us see wet areas you can't spot by eye.

**Moisture survey:** We use moisture meters to check the roof in many spots. This confirms where the roof is wet and how far the water has spread.
