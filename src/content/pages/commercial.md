## Commercial roof leaks cost more the longer they wait

A leak over a home is a hassle. A leak over a store, office or apartment is a business problem. Water can ruin stock, shut down a suite, upset tenants and lead to mold claims. The sooner it's found, the cheaper it is to fix.

Common signs on commercial buildings:

- Stained or falling ceiling tiles
- Water near HVAC units, skylights or roof hatches
- Tenant complaints about drips or musty smells
- Standing water on the roof long after a storm
- Blisters, splits or loose seams you can see from a roof hatch

## What we repair on commercial roofs

Most commercial buildings have a [flat roof](/flat-roof-leak-repair/): TPO, modified bitumen, built-up or coated. Warehouses and shops often have [metal roofs](/metal-roof-leak-repair/). Condos, townhomes and HOA buildings usually have shingle or tile. We fix leaks on all of them.

We also repair the details where most commercial leaks start: [roof drains, scuppers and flashing](/roof-flashing-leak-repair/), HVAC curbs, pipe boots and parapet walls.
