## What drives the cost of a roof leak repair

No honest roofer can give you a firm price without seeing the roof. Here's what changes the cost:

- **The cause.** A cracked pipe boot or a few missing shingles is a small job. A leak that has been soaking the roof for months is a bigger one.
- **Hidden damage.** Water can rot the wood deck on sloped roofs or soak the insulation on flat roofs. Damaged material has to be cut out and replaced.
- **Roof type.** Shingle, tile, metal and flat roofs each use different materials and methods. Tile takes the most care because each tile has to be lifted and reset.
- **Where the leak is.** Leaks around chimneys, skylights, valleys and AC units take more detail work than a hole in the open roof.
- **Height and access.** Steep pitches, two-story homes and hard-to-reach spots add time and safety gear.
- **Timing.** Emergency and after-hours calls can cost more than a scheduled repair.

## Why waiting costs more

A small leak gets bigger. Each rain soaks more wood or insulation. In Orlando, that can happen almost every day in summer. A repair that would have been quick in May can turn into rotted decking, mold and ceiling damage by August.

## Repair or replace?

**Repair usually makes sense when:**

- The leak is in one area or at one detail
- The roof still has years of life left
- Damage under the surface is limited to a small area

**Replacement may make sense when:**

- The roof leaks in many places
- Large areas are rotted or wet under the surface
- The roof is old and repairs keep failing
