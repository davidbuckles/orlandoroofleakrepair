## What drives the cost of a flat roof leak repair

No honest roofer can give you a price without seeing the roof. Here's what changes the cost:

- **The cause.** A loose seam or a cracked drain seal is a small job. A leak that has been soaking the roof for months is a bigger one.
- **Wet insulation.** Water under the roof soaks into the insulation. Wet insulation has to be cut out and replaced, which adds labor and material.
- **Roof type.** TPO, modified bitumen and built-up roofs each use different materials and methods.
- **Where the leak is.** Leaks around HVAC units, drains and walls take more detail work than a hole in the open roof.
- **Access.** Tall buildings, no roof hatch, or hard-to-reach spots add time.
- **Timing.** Emergency and after-hours calls can cost more than a scheduled repair.

## Why waiting costs more

A small leak gets bigger. Water spreads under the roof and soaks more insulation every time it rains. In Orlando, that can happen almost every day in summer. A repair that would have been quick in May can turn into a large one by August. Mold and interior damage add even more cost.

## Repair or replace?

**Repair usually makes sense when:**

- The leak is in one area or at one detail
- The roof still has years of life left
- Wet insulation is limited to a small area

**Replacement may make sense when:**

- The roof leaks in many places
- Large areas are wet under the surface
- The roof is old and repairs keep failing

A [moisture survey and leak detection](/flat-roof-leak-detection/) shows how much of the roof is wet, so you can make this choice with real facts. We will always tell you honestly which option we'd choose for our own building.
