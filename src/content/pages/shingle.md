## Signs of a shingle roof leak

- **Missing or lifted shingles**, often after a storm
- **Shingles curling, cracking** or losing their granules
- **Granules in your gutters** like coarse sand
- **Stains on the ceiling** below a bathroom or kitchen vent
- **Damp wood or insulation** in the attic
- **Daylight** showing through the roof deck from the attic

## Where shingle roofs leak

**Pipe boots.** The rubber seal around plumbing vents is the number one cause of shingle roof leaks in Orlando. The sun cracks it, and water runs down the pipe.

**Valleys.** Valleys are where two slopes meet. They carry more water than any other part of the roof, so worn shingles or open seams here show up fast.

**Wind damage.** Summer storms and hurricanes can lift or tear off shingles. Once the seal breaks, the next wind can take more.

**Flashing.** Where the roof meets a wall, chimney or skylight, metal flashing keeps water out. Loose or rusted flashing lets it in. See [flashing and vent leak repair](/roof-flashing-leak-repair/).

**Nail pops.** Nails can back out over time and push up the shingle above them, leaving a hole.
