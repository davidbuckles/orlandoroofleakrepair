## Signs your modified bitumen roof is leaking

- **Cracked or open laps** where the rolls overlap
- **Blisters**, especially ones that are soft, split or growing
- **Bare spots** where the granule surface has worn away
- **Alligator cracking**, a pattern of small cracks in the surface
- **Loose flashing** at walls and roof edges
- **Ceiling stains** inside the building

## Torch-down roofs in Florida heat

Modified bitumen holds up well, but Florida sun is tough on asphalt. Year after year, heat dries it out. Daily storms then push water into any crack. That's why many mod bit leaks start at the laps and flashing first.

## How we repair mod bit leaks

**Cracked laps:** We clean and heat the lap, then re-seal it. If it's too far gone, we add a strip of new material over it.

**Blisters:** We cut open damaged blisters, let them dry, and patch them with new modified bitumen.

**Worn surface:** We patch bare or cracked areas. If wear is widespread, a coating may add life to the roof. We will tell you honestly if it's time to think about replacement instead.
