## Signs of a metal roof leak

- **Ceiling stains** that grow after heavy or windy rain
- **Rust streaks** around screws or along panel edges
- **Screws that are loose**, backed out or missing
- **Bent or lifted panels** or trim after a storm
- **Drips along the walls** of a warehouse or shop

## Where metal roofs leak

**Screws and washers.** A metal roof can have thousands of screws. Each rubber washer slowly breaks down in the sun. When enough fail, water gets in at many small spots.

**Seams and laps.** Where panels overlap, sealant can dry out and gaps can open as the metal moves with the heat.

**Trim and ridge caps.** Wind can lift trim at the edges and the ridge, and rain blows in underneath.

**Penetrations.** Vents, pipes and HVAC units cut through the panels. The boots and flashing around them are common leak points. See [flashing and vent leak repair](/roof-flashing-leak-repair/).

**Rust.** Scratches, standing water and salt air all start rust. Left alone, it eats through the metal.
